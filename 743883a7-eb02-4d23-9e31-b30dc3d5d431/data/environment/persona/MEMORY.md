# Memory: Brittany Morales

## Personal Profile
Brittany Morales is a Cuban-American residential real estate agent who grew up on her abuela's recipes and built a career on hustle, image, and relationships. She holds a BS in Business Administration from the University of South Florida (2006) and has been a licensed Florida agent since 2012. She is energetic and driven, persuasive, competitive about her numbers, organized to the extreme with color-coded calendars and follow-up systems, and an empathetic listener who understands that buying a home is an emotional act. She is image-conscious, always put together, and resilient. She has lost deals that crushed her and bounced back the next morning. Her cultural identity runs through her cooking, her family, and the Cuban coffee that starts every day.
