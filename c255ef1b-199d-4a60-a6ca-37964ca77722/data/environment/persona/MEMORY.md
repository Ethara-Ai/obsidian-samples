# Memory: Margaret Edwards

## Personal Profile

- **Emergency contact:** James Murphy (partner, cohabiting). Secondary: Dorothy Edwards (mother).
- Documentary filmmaker, co-founder of Lamplighter Films. Has been making documentaries professionally for over a decade.
- Education: BA English and Film Studies, Trinity College Dublin (2012). MA Documentary Practice, National Film School at IADT Dun Laoghaire (2014).
- Languages: English (native), French (conversational, from a gap year in Paris, used on European co-productions), basic Spanish (evening classes and travel, long intending to deepen it).
- Citizenship: Irish. Born in Galway, partly raised there and partly in Dublin; in Dublin since university. Soft Dublin accent with absorbed Galway.
- Personality: layered, observation-trained attention; listens before speaking after years of documentary interviews. Quiet stubbornness under an easygoing surface. Loyal in ways that occasionally cost her professionally. Refuses to make a film about a community she has not earned the right to enter.
- Carries a low-grade money anxiety since film school. Documentary work is feast-or-famine; grants and festival pay are unreliable. Manages the precarity with a discipline James has gradually adopted.
- Culturally Catholic, not religious. Does not attend Mass. Closest she has to a sacred practice is the Sunday morning newspaper-and-coffee ritual with James and the Saturday Phoenix Park loop.
- Philosophy: documentary is an ethical practice as much as an artistic one, and slow work matters more than fast. Holds herself and her collaborators to standards that have cost her opportunities and likely saved her career.
- Comfort and decompression: Phoenix Park run first, then the kitchen. Cooking is medicine on hard days; James reads it as a signal and stops asking direct questions those evenings.
- Has not been outside Europe in years and is not motivated to fix it. A long-discussed West of Ireland road trip with James (Connemara, the islands) waits for a quiet stretch between projects.

## Work & Projects

- **Lamplighter Films**: small production collective co-founded with Claire Nicholson in 2020. Shared studio in Smithfield, Dublin. Focus: social-issue documentaries, local stories, European co-productions. Sixth anniversary October 2026.
- **Title**: Director and Producer. Directs and produces her own projects, occasionally produces for other collective members.
- **"The Leaving"**: feature documentary on emigration through three families across three generations. In post-production; rough cut complete, fine cut in progress. Targeting autumn 2026 festival premiere. Three years of work; first screening outside the edit suite is the October 3 rough cut session.
- **"Still Waters"**: short documentary commissioned by Channel 4 about a rural Roscommon community fighting a water infrastructure project. Pre-production phase, shoot scheduled autumn 2026. Helen Brennan is the commissioning editor.
- **Danish co-production (in development)**: early-stage collaboration with a Danish production company on a documentary about climate refugees in the North Atlantic. Research and funding application phase; Creative Europe MEDIA funding being assessed.
- **Schedule shape**: flexible and project-dependent. Editing at the Smithfield studio or home. Production days on location. Admin and grant writing from home. No fixed office hours; structure around project needs.
- **Professional development**: applying to Berlinale Talents 2027 (application due autumn 2026). Building European co-production network. Personal essay project as a written companion to "The Leaving."

## Interests & Hobbies

- **Filmmaking-adjacent reading and viewing**: not a hobby; the hours spent on documentary theory, films from other traditions, and projects in long development are work that has become inseparable from how she organises her life.
- **Running**: Phoenix Park three mornings a week (Mon, Wed, Sat). Same route, comfortable pace, podcast in ears. Partly stress management, partly slow processing, partly restoring her own scale after long stretches in dim edit rooms.
- **Documentary photography**: smaller hobby that has been quietly growing. Carries a film camera on personal walks and travel. Does not post the photographs. Develops the rolls at a shop near Smithfield and keeps prints in a small box she has been gradually filling for years.
- **Reading**: documentary theory and European film criticism on the work desk; literary fiction on the bedside table, particularly Irish women's writing she came to late; long-form journalism on weekends.
- **Yoga and swimming**: body-maintenance disciplines made necessary by long editing days. Came to love them slowly.
- **Cooking**: weekend pleasure. Weeknight cooking is practical (batch soups, stews, pasta). Sunday roast tradition with James.

## Preferences

- **Diet**: flexitarian, mostly plant-based at home. Eats meat occasionally when dining out or at family gatherings. No allergies (but cooks gluten-free at home for James).
- **Comfort foods**: mother's shepherd's pie, a particular brown bread, childhood lemon drizzle cake, sourdough with butter, fish and chips from a proper chipper, a good roast dinner.
- **Regular spots**: The Fumbally (brunch, Stoneybatter area), Assassination Custard (coffee and pastries on Manor Street, morning ritual, loyal for six years), Brother Hubbard (Middle Eastern fusion, dinner with James), Galway Bay Seafood (when visiting parents).
- **Coffee**: 3 cups daily. Strong flat whites. Assassination Custard is the standard.
- **News and reading**: *The Irish Times* every morning. *Sight & Sound*, *Filmmaker Magazine*, *Documentary.org*. Tracks arts policy and European film funding landscape closely.
- **Music**: English folk, post-punk, ambient electronic, film scores. Fontaines D.C. a favourite.
- **Podcasts**: *The Documentary Podcast* (BBC World Service), *Pure Nonfiction*, *Desert Island Discs*. Listens during runs.
- **Reading taste**: documentary theory, literary fiction (Irish women's writing especially), European cinema criticism, long-form journalism.
- **Gifts**: books, records, or experiences (cinema memberships, exhibition tickets, a good bottle of wine). For family, something handmade or thoughtfully chosen. For James on birthdays: a hunted-down piece of vintage music gear.
- **Shopping**: shops carefully with a long horizon. Pays full price for camera equipment, proper running shoes, the few good books she will reread. Refuses to pay full price for almost anything else. Buys clothes only when current ones wear through.
- **Travel taste**: small European cities over large ones. Likes train travel. Specific affection for Berlin in autumn, Vienna in winter, Lisbon in spring.
- **Aesthetic**: natural materials, durable construction, objects with a clear use.
- **Likes**: the first quiet hour of morning, Dublin winter light along the Liffey, commissioning editors who actually read the treatments, festival programmers who follow up, older women in her field who treat her as a colleague, a Saturday film screening followed by a long unhurried dinner.
- **Pet peeves**: broadcasters who treat documentary as glorified TV journalism, funders who confuse application volume for quality, "Irish documentary scene" used by visitors as if it were one thing, subjects who renegotiate consent after the edit, loud restaurants, phones during screenings, anyone who explains her field to her at parties.
- **Sensory world**: sight-anchored; notices the light at every hour and stores smells precisely (morning coffee, cinema lobby, peat smoke at her parents' in winter). Comfort layering at home: a record on low while cooking, James's music from the next room, the Sunday kitchen radio. True silence unsettles her, except on the morning park run where silence is the point.
