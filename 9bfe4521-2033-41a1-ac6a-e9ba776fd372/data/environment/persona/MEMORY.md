# Memory: Reggie Clayton

## Personal Profile

Reggie Clayton teaches industrial arts at Ridgeway Regional High School in Memphis, serves as shop steward for Memphis Education Association Local 780, and runs a custom welding and fabrication side practice out of his back-alley garage. Born and raised in Orange Mound, where he still lives a few blocks from the rowhouse he grew up in, he came to teaching after sixteen years welding the mill floor at Rivermark Steel ended with the 2018 plant closure; he holds a Welding & Fabrication Certificate from Shelby Technical College (2001) and a BA in Education from Delta State University (2019), earned on emergency cert. Divorced from Tamika Clayton since 2022 after marrying in 2010, he keeps alternating week-on/week-off custody of Marcus (12) and Zuri (9) and is the primary family decision-maker for his mother Dorothy's care in Whitehaven, where her wet macular degeneration and rheumatoid arthritis sit in every Sunday-morning calculation. African American with deep Memphis roots — Dorothy moved from Selma in 1965 during the Great Migration, his father Curtis came up out of South Memphis before leaving for Atlanta when Reggie was nineteen — he was raised Baptist and still drives Dorothy to Sunday service at Greater Mount Zion Baptist Church. He speaks English natively and is comfortable in working-class Southern vernacular. He carries his identity as a working-class Memphis Black man, a union shop steward, and a divorced co-parent as a lifelong condition rather than a balancing act, absorbs pressure rather than performing it, and runs practical to the point of plain — quietly sentimental around the kids, the F-100, and the people who pulled him through Rivermark.

## Work & Projects

- Role: Industrial Arts Teacher at Ridgeway Regional High School, Memphis City Schools.
- Tenure: 6 years at Ridgeway Regional (since 2020). Previously 16 years at Rivermark Steel until the 2018 plant closure. Bridged 2018–2020 with substitute and shop-floor side work while finishing the Delta State BA (2019) and starting at Ridgeway on emergency cert.
- Education: Orange Mound High (2000), Welding & Fabrication Certificate from Shelby Technical College (2001), BA in Education from Delta State University (2019) via alternative certification while teaching on emergency cert.
- Reports to Principal Denise Patterson.
- Teaches: Introduction to Woodworking (9th), Metalworking & Welding I (10th to 11th), Advanced Fabrication (11th to 12th, his favorite). 5 periods per day, about 120 students, class size 18 to 22.
- Shop steward, Memphis Education Association Local 780. Grievance handling, workload negotiation, monthly union meetings.
- Active fight: district wants to cut the Advanced Fabrication section due to low enrollment (14 students). Reggie wrote a proposal showing job placement rates for shop graduates. Next round during fall budget cycle.
- Side income: custom welding and fabrication jobs, $200 to $500 per job, 2 to 3 jobs per month through word of mouth. Cash or Venmo. Railings, gates, furniture frames, repair work.
- Summer: 3-week welding camp through the district summer enrichment program ($3,200 stipend).

## Interests & Hobbies

- Truck restoration. 1972 Ford F-100 Ranger in the back garage. Original inline-6 swapped for a 302 V8 pulled from a junkyard Explorer. Body primered, working on interior and wiring harness. Two-year project, currently in year four.
- BBQ and smoking. Reverse-flow smoker welded from a 250-gallon propane tank. Brisket (14-hour cooks), pulled pork, ribs. Local competitions with Darnell as Smoke Ring BBQ. The team built out a small online storefront in 2024: house rubs, a bottled sauce line, and a small branded-merch run. Darnell's nephew (software engineer in his late twenties) handles the build and operations; Reggie and Darnell own the recipe IP, run the cooks, and ship orders out of the back garage.
- Homebrewing. Brews every other Saturday with Darnell. IPAs mostly, the occasional stout. 5-gallon batches.
- Youth baseball coaching. Assistant coach for Marcus's travel team, Orange Mound Tigers (12U). Games Sat AM, practice Wed PM.
- Reading. WWII Pacific theater, Stoic philosophy (Marcus Aurelius, Epictetus), Memphis labor history. Porch and a beer on summer evenings.
- Fishing. Wolf River or Shelby Farms Lake with Marcus and Zuri on occasional weekends. More about time together than the fish.
